人

About

I am an applied economist working at the intersection of urban, environmental, and real estate economics. My research asks how China's built environment — its housing markets, transport networks, and energy systems — shapes and is shaped by public policy, and what the country's green transition means for firms, workers, and families.

I hold a PhD in Applied Economics from Cornell University and a B.A. in Finance with a B.S. in Statistics from Peking University. My work has been cited 2,700+ times (Google Scholar), and I am listed among the World's Top 2% Scientists in Economics (2024, 2025) and IDEAS/RePEc's top 1% of female economists by publications over the past decade. I received the AsREAP Women Achievement Award in 2026.

I serve as Co-Editor of the Journal of Environmental Economics and Management and of China Economic Review. I also run Impactful Research, a platform devoted to cultivating research taste among young scholars in China.

研

Research

Environmental Economics

碳中和 · 空气污染与治理

The economics of carbon neutrality — decarbonizing buildings, transportation, and enterprises — together with air pollution's economic consequences and the governance structures that succeed, or fail, in controlling it.

Urban Economics

交通 · 房地产市场

How transport infrastructure and housing and land markets shape China's cities — where people and firms locate, how prices form, and who gains from the geography that results.

Public Policy & Labor

公共政策与劳动力

How public policy moves labor markets, household decisions, and demographic outcomes — from migration restrictions and fertility policy to civil-service reform.
